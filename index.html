For My Bossy Girl

This silly boy feels so much for you.

I’m sorry I let my fears speak louder than all the love you show me. I pulled away because I was afraid I wasn’t important to you, when what I should have done was talk to you, listen to you, and trust your words.

It wasn’t fair of me to make you feel as though your love and everything you do for me weren’t enough. I’m sorry if the way I acted hurt you. The last thing I ever want is to hurt the person who has done so much for me, shared so many firsts with me, and holds such a special place in my life.

I understand that you need quiet moments, space, and time to yourself. I want to learn to love you in that way too—with calm, trust, and without making you feel pressured.

Even though my actions may sometimes seem to say otherwise, and I end up complaining more than I would like, the truth is that my heart holds so much love and affection for you, my beautiful girl. I don’t want my insecurities to hide all the affection, admiration, and gratitude I carry for you.

Thank you for talking to me with so much love, even when my own thoughts made me doubt. Thank you for reminding me how special I am to you and for continuing to build this with me. Your words meant more to me than you probably realize.

Just as I promised to trust what you tell me, I want you to never doubt what I feel for you. I love you deeply. Even on my insecure days, even when the distance feels heavy and my mind gets too loud, my heart keeps choosing you.

I can’t promise to be perfect, but I can promise to make an effort to listen, to talk before pulling away, to respect your space, and to take better care of this beautiful love we share. I want you to feel safe, at ease, and deeply loved with me.

You don’t have to reply right now. I just wanted to find a beautiful way to remind you how much I love you and how sorry I am that I ever made you feel otherwise.

With all my love, Max
♥
